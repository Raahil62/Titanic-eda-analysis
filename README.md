# KEY INSIGHT
1) Gender had a strong relationship with survival: 74.2% of female passengers survived compared with only 18.9% of male passengers.
2) Passenger class strongly influenced survival: First-class passengers had a 63.0% survival rate, compared with 47.3% for second class and 24.2% for third class.
3) Most passengers travelled in third class: 491 of the 891 passengers (55.1%) were in third class, making it the largest passenger group.
4) First-class passengers paid substantially higher fares: Their average fare was 84.15, compared with 20.66 for second class and 13.68 for third class.
5) Fare values were highly spread out: The average fare was 32.20, while the maximum was 512.33, indicating the presence of very high fare values/outliers.
6) Survivors were slightly younger on average: Survivors had an average age of 28.34 years compared with 30.63 years for non-survivors. However, the difference is relatively small.
7) The dataset initially had significant missing data: Age had 177 missing values, Cabin had 687, and Embarked had 2. We addressed these during data cleaning.
8) No duplicate records were found: The dataset contained 891 unique rows based on the duplicate-row check.
